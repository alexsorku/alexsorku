# Investing.com: Traffic & Audience Analysis

_Compiled October 2026 from public third-party estimates (Similarweb, Semrush, Ahrefs) and Investing.com's own published figures. All traffic numbers are modeled estimates, so use them for direction and channel mix, not as exact counts._

## 1. Traffic volume

| Metric | Similarweb (Jul 2026) | Semrush (Aug 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly visits | ~145.6M (3-month total as listed) | 125.3M (Jun 148.2M → Jul 135.9M → Aug 125.3M) |
| Trend | −3.0% month over month | −7.8% month over month |
| Global rank | #378 | — |
| Visit duration | 4:33 | 10:10 |
| Pages / visit | 2.90 | — |
| Bounce rate | — | 63.7% |

Ahrefs estimates organic search traffic at ~87.7M across ~785K ranking keywords.

**Takeaway:** a top-400 global site with roughly 125–145M visits a month. Traffic fell over summer 2026, which fits a cooling market-news cycle.

## 2. Main traffic source: **Direct**

| Channel | Share |
|---|---|
| **Direct** | **55% of desktop visits (Similarweb); 68.5% of all traffic (Semrush)** |
| Organic search (Google) | Second place; google.com supplies 21.9% (Semrush). 98.9% of search traffic is organic and only 1.1% is paid |
| Organic social | Third (Similarweb) |
| AI assistants | Small but new: ChatGPT ~109K, Gemini ~61K, Perplexity ~16K visits a month |

Why direct traffic dominates:
- Investing.com is a **daily-habit utility**. People bookmark the economic calendar, live quotes, futures, FX rates and charts and check them several times a day.
- Its **mobile app** (130M+ claimed downloads) drives repeat use.
- **Brand search acts like direct traffic.** The top search keyword is simply "investing".
- **Search is the acquisition engine.** It ranks for ticker and quote queries such as "meta stock", "gold price" and "USD/TRY", plus news. That is how new users find the site before they turn into direct visitors.

## 3. Who the users are

**Geography (very international, ~44 language editions):**
- Semrush (Aug 2026): **Turkey #1 at 20.4%** (~25.6M visits), followed by Italy, Spain, Brazil, and the **US at ~6.3%**.
- Similarweb (older snapshot): US 8.1%, Italy 7.9%, Russia 6.8%, India 6.4%, with ~60% spread across the long tail.
- Turkey's large share likely reflects high retail interest in FX, gold and inflation hedging (USD/TRY, gold prices). The site's localized editions (tr., it., es., br.) rank strongly in local search.

**Demographics (Similarweb):**
- **70% male / 30% female**
- Largest age group: **25–34**, followed by 35–44

**Behavioral profile:**
- **Active retail traders and self-directed investors**, mostly in FX, stocks, indices, commodities, and crypto. They check quotes, calendars and technical summaries during market hours.
- **Finance professionals and semi-professionals**, including brokers, analysts, and economists who use the economic calendar and historical data.
- **News readers** following macro events such as Fed and CPI releases, earnings, and geopolitics.
- **Paying users:** ~300K InvestingPro subscribers (company figure, possibly dated) out of ~46–50M claimed monthly users. Most users never pay, so the business still relies mainly on ads and broker lead generation.

## 4. Competitive set

Similarweb, Semrush and Ahrefs place it alongside **Yahoo Finance** (closest by similarity), **TradingView** (closest by Semrush), MarketWatch, CNBC and Investopedia, plus local brokers such as gcmyatirim.com.tr and getmidas.com in Turkey.

## 5. Summary

- **Main traffic source:** Direct (55–68%), followed by Google organic search (~22%). Social, referral and paid traffic are minor.
- **Users:** a global, mostly male (70%) audience of active retail traders and investors aged 25–44. They are concentrated in Turkey and Southern Europe (Italy, Spain), along with Brazil and the US, and they come back daily for quotes, charts, the economic calendar, and market news.
